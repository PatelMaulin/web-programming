Why I Created This Website

My name is Maulin Patel, and I created this website to share my interest in cricket. I wanted to make a simple place where visitors could learn about the game, its history, and some well-known players. My goal was to explain the topic in a way that is easy for beginners to understand.

What Motivated Me

I enjoy cricket because of the teamwork, focus, and strategy involved in the game. My interest in the sport motivated me to choose it as the topic for this project. I wanted to turn something I enjoy into a website that other people could explore and learn from, especially people who are new to cricket.

What I Am Learning

I also created this website to practice HTML and CSS for my web development class. This project helps me learn how to organize content, connect pages with links, and use an external stylesheet. I chose a simple black, white, and gray design so visitors can focus on the information while I continue improving my coding skills.
