03 / THE BASICS

Welcome to
your first innings.

Score runs

Batters score by running between the wickets or hitting boundaries. A ball reaching the boundary after touching the ground scores four; clearing it without bouncing scores six.

Take wickets

The fielding side tries to dismiss batters. Common dismissals include bowled, caught, leg before wicket, and run out.

Count overs

An over contains six legal deliveries. Wides and no-balls add extras and must be bowled again.
