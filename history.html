Cricket started in England many years ago. Over time, the rules became more organized and the sport became more popular.

Cricket later spread to many countries around the world. Countries such as India, Australia, South Africa, and Pakistan became very strong cricket nations.

Today, cricket is played in Test matches, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20 matches. These formats make the game interesting for different types of fans.
