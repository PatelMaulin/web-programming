About

Cricket is a sport played between two teams of eleven players. It is played with a bat and ball on a large field. The main goal is to score more runs than the other team.

The game has batting, bowling, and fielding. A batter tries to hit the ball and score runs. A bowler tries to get the batter out.

Cricket is very popular in countries such as India, England, Australia, Pakistan, South Africa, Sri Lanka, and New Zealand.

Basic Cricket Facts

  • Each team normally has eleven players.
  • Batters score runs.
  • Bowlers try to take wickets.
  • Cricket has different match formats.
